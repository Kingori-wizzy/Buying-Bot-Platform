# BRS — Business Requirements Specification

## Purpose

Holds **Business Requirements Specifications** that define _why_ the platform exists and _what business outcomes_ it must deliver, independent of technical design.

## Scope

- Business goals, stakeholders, and success metrics
- Omnichannel commerce capabilities and constraints
- AI-assisted buying outcomes from a business perspective
- Regulatory, operational, and commercial requirements
- Prioritized epics and business rules (non-technical)

## Alignment

BRS content feeds the SRS and must remain consistent with the Enterprise Architecture Document in [`../EAD/`](../EAD/) and [`../ARCHITECTURE.md`](../ARCHITECTURE.md).
